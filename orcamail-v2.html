❓ How to Use OrcaMail

🐬 Welcome to OrcaMail
OrcaMail lets you send private, encrypted messages between crypto wallets. Any EVM wallet works — no new wallet needed. Subject and body are encrypted on your device, so only the recipient can read them. Opting in and receiving are free; unlimited sending uses a small LCAI subscription on Lightchain.
🚀 Getting Started
Connect your Trust Wallet, or any EVM wallet using the Connect Wallet button. Once connected, tap Opt In — this registers your wallet so others can send you messages. Opting in is free and doesn't require a transaction.
📬 Receiving Messages
Receiving is always free. Once opted in, anyone can send you a message. Open your Inbox tab to see messages. Tap any message to read it — it decrypts right in your browser using your wallet key, so nobody else can see the content. Links in a message are clickable and open in a new tab.

Tap ↩ Reply to answer — it fills in the sender and quotes their message. Tap the sender address to copy it. Use the 🔎 search box at the top of your inbox to find a message by sender or subject.
📤 Sending Messages
Go to the Compose tab. In the To box, type a saved contact's name (it autocompletes) or paste a wallet address (0x...) and press Enter — each recipient becomes a removable chip. You can add several people at once, or use ➕ Add a whole group to drop in everyone in a group. Add a subject and your message, then tap Send Encrypted Message — OrcaMail encrypts a separate copy for each recipient so only they can read it. You can also press Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter on Mac) to send.

Tap 😀 Emoji to add emojis, and misspelled words are underlined as you type so you can catch typos.

You get 5 free sends after connecting. After that, a subscription of about $0.50/month in LCAI (never below the on-chain minimum) unlocks unlimited sending for 30 days.
📎 Attachments
In Compose, tap 📎 Attach to send photos, screenshots, gifs, or documents. Each file can be up to 3 MB, up to 6 MB total per message. Attachments are encrypted end-to-end just like your message — only the recipient can open them. Images and gifs show right inside the message; documents arrive as a download button.
👥 Contacts & Groups
The Contacts tab is your private address book. Save people by name so you don't have to remember wallet addresses, and sort them into groups like Friends or Work. When composing, type a name instead of an address, or send to a whole group at once. Use Export / Import to back up your list.

Your contacts stay private on your device — they're saved only in your browser and are never uploaded to the server.
💳 Subscribing
After your 5 free sends, go to Settings and tap Subscribe. The app prices ~$0.50 USD in LCAI (live rate), but never below the contract minimum. Approve subscribe() on Lightchain — active once confirmed.
🔒 Your Privacy
Subject and body are encrypted with ECIES before they leave your device. The server stores ciphertext and routing addresses only — it cannot read content. Wallet addresses are public identifiers used for delivery.

🔐 Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026  ·  Built for Lightchain · Unofficial community tool · KeikoDev

1. What OrcaMail Is

OrcaMail is a wallet-to-wallet encrypted messaging service built on the Lightchain blockchain. It is operated by an individual developer and provided as-is for the benefit of the Lightchain community.

2. What Data We Collect

When you use OrcaMail, the following data is stored on our server:

  • Wallet addresses — your Ethereum-compatible public address, used to route messages to your inbox. Wallet addresses are pseudonymous and are not linked to your real-world identity by this service.
  • Encrypted message blobs — ciphertext of subject + body (ECIES). We cannot decrypt message content.
  • Public keys — secp256k1 public key from opt-in (not a private key).
  • Opt-in records — whether your wallet is registered to receive mail.
  • Send counts — free-tier counters per wallet address.
  • Auth signatures — short-lived signed messages used to prove wallet control for send/opt-in/opt-out (not stored long-term as identity documents).

We do not collect your legal name, phone number, or personal email. Hosting providers may process standard technical metadata (e.g. IP addresses in access logs) under their own terms — we do not use that for marketing or identity profiling.

3. End-to-End Encryption

Subject and body are encrypted on your device with ECIES before upload. The server stores ciphertext and cannot read content. Only the recipient can decrypt. From/to wallet addresses are visible for routing (they are not secret on a public chain ecosystem).

4. Where Data Is Stored

Data is stored on a third-party cloud infrastructure provider (currently Railway). It is not stored on any personal device belonging to the operator. The cloud provider may have its own data processing terms which apply to infrastructure-level operations.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Messages remain stored until you delete them from your inbox. Opt-in and public key records remain until you opt out. Send count records are retained for service integrity. You can remove your data at any time by opting out in the Settings tab, which deletes your opt-in record and public key from our server.
